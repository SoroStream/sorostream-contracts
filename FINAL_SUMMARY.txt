ISSUE #3: Atomic Renewal Failure ✅
───────────────────────────────────
  File: contracts/streaming/src/test.rs
  Test: test_renew_fails_atomically_on_insufficient_allowance()
  Lines: 498–549
  
  What it does:
    • Creates auto-renewing stream with 1_000 token deposit
    • Sets sender's allowance to only 500 tokens (insufficient)
    • Attempts renewal and verifies it fails
    • Confirms NO partial execution:
      - Recipient doesn't receive vested tokens
      - Stream is NOT renewed
  
  Why it matters:
    • Verifies atomicity (all-or-nothing behavior)
    • Prevents token leakage on failed renewal
    • Protects keeper safety (can't partially drain allowance)
